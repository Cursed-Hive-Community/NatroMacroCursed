# Leaving is not the same as crashing.
#
# A macro that drops its connection stays in the roster, offline. One that
# says LEAVE is struck off it, file included.

# the seats go in before the coordinator reads the roster,
# rather than relying on it to notice a reload
seat 1 main bench main
seat 2 fuzzy bench fuzzy
coordinator start
wait 2500
connect all
wait 2000
clear all

say --- row 2 drops its connection ---
disconnect 2
wait 6000
say row 2 should still be in the panel, marked offline

say --- now it leaves on purpose ---
connect 2
wait 2000
leave 2
wait 6000
expect 1 GONE row=2
say and it should be out of the roster table entirely
